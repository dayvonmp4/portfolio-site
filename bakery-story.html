Our Story

How a home kitchen in Portland became a neighborhood staple.

A Bakery Born
from Passion

Flour & Story started in a home kitchen in 2018 when founder Claire Navarro began selling sourdough loaves to neighbors. What started as a hobby became a calling.

Within a year, Claire had a waitlist. By 2020 she opened a small storefront on Maple Street. Today our team of six bakes everything by hand each morning before sunrise.

We partner with local farms, use organic flour whenever possible, and believe that good food made with care is always worth it. No shortcuts, no preservatives, just honest craft.

What We Believe

01

Craft Over Convenience

Every loaf is slow-fermented, every croissant laminated by hand. We never cut corners to move faster.

02

Local First

We source from Oregon farms whenever possible and change our menu with the seasons.

03

The Neighborhood Matters

We donate day-old bread to local shelters every evening. A good bakery feeds more than appetites.
